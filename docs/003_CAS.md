# ADR-0003: Content Addressed Storage

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Initially, every chunk was assigned a randomly generated UUID.

Although UUIDs uniquely identified chunks, they provided no relationship between the identifier and the underlying data.

This prevented transparent deduplication and resumable uploads.

## Decision

Chunk identifiers are now computed as:

SHA256(chunk_data)

The hash becomes the chunk's unique identifier.

## Why

A content-derived identifier allows PebbleFS to:

- Detect duplicate chunks.
- Reuse existing physical data.
- Eliminate a separate checksum field.
- Verify integrity during reads.
- Simplify replication.
- Support future deduplication.

## Previous Design

Chunk

↓

UUID

↓

Stored

## Current Design

Chunk Data

↓

SHA256

↓

Chunk ID

↓

Stored

## Consequences

Chunk identity is now deterministic.

Identical chunks always produce identical IDs.

Different chunks are overwhelmingly unlikely to collide.
